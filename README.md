# Featured Projects

### IAM Governance & Workforce Access Automation Platform

A PowerShell-based identity governance platform that models enterprise workforce access across a synthetic population of **10,000 employees**.

The platform implements **Joiner-Mover-Leaver automation, RBAC, policy-based access controls, Segregation of Duties, Okta provisioning, access certification, access-drift detection, remediation, orphan-account handling, and audit logging**.

The project started with a question I've become increasingly interested in:

**How do you determine what an identity should have access to — and continuously verify that it still should?**

I'm also exploring how these governance concepts can extend beyond workforce identities to **service accounts, non-human identities, and AI agents**.

### DocTime

A healthcare appointment scheduling application built with **Python and Streamlit**, modeling patient and provider workflows including authentication, provider discovery, availability, appointment booking, dashboards, and account management.

### U.S. Housing Market Analysis

A **SQL and time-series analysis** project exploring nearly two decades of U.S. housing and economic data, including affordability, supply and demand, interest rates, income, elasticity, and broader market trends.
